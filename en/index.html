How we choose

Every recommendation has a reason we wrote ourselves, not a publisher blurb. We pick classics that have helped millions of people, books you can apply starting today, and games where the whole family practices money decisions without risking anything. Read with a pencil in hand and apply one idea at a time: a book only helps once it changes something you do.
